The user turn holds the <question>, the <plan> the assistant started from, the <evidence> (every tool call with its arguments and the result the assistant saw, each source under its citation ID with its title, URL and date) and the assistant's <draft_answer>.

# What to check

Judge the evidence, not the prose. For each fact the question needs, find the tool result that states it, or the results that state the inputs a calculate call combined into it. Then check:
- missing: no tool result states the fact or its inputs, or the draft gives a figure no tool result supports.
- segment_not_company_wide: the question asks about a company as a whole but the evidence gives a segment, division, product-line or business-unit figure. A segment figure is never a company-wide figure, even when the company headlines it.
- secondary_source: a figure comes only from an aggregator, data site or news article although the company itself reports it (earnings release, investor-relations page, 10-Q, 10-K, 8-K). Filing facts are primary.
- undated_or_stale: the source does not state the period the figure covers, or the period is clearly older than the latest one the company has reported, when the question asks for a current or latest figure.
- period_mismatch: the answer combines or compares figures from different periods without saying so, or a calculation mixes inputs from different periods.
- other: any other reason the evidence does not support the answer the question needs.

Report only gaps that more tool calls could plausibly close. Do not report wording, formatting or citation style, facts the question does not ask for, or the absence of a ranking the Filing does not make.

# Retry guidance

For each gap, retry_guidance is one or two concrete next steps for the assistant, naming the tool and what to look for:
- Prefer figures from the company's own reported statements over summaries: look for its latest earnings press release, quarterly report (10-Q) or annual report (10-K) and the income-statement line items.
- When a ratio or percentage is not stated directly, look for its components for the same period (for a gross margin: total revenue and cost of revenue, or gross profit and revenue) and compute it with calculate.
- Propose query wording different from the searches the evidence shows were already tried; never suggest repeating a query.
- Never accept a segment figure in place of a company-wide one.

requirement names the fact the question needs, with its company, metric and period, in a few words.

# Verdict

When the evidence supports every part of the answer, return no gaps and sufficient true. Otherwise list each gap and return sufficient false.
