## calculate
Evaluate an arithmetic expression exactly and return {"expression", "result"}. Use it for every
computation in an answer (percentages, ratios, differences, growth rates) instead of doing the
arithmetic yourself, so the calculation is visible and checkable.

Allowed: plain numbers, + - * / ** %, unary minus, parentheses, round(x) or round(x, ndigits),
and abs(x). Nothing else is accepted: no variables, names, other functions or comparisons.
Write numbers without thousands separators, currency symbols, units or percent signs, and use
the same unit for every figure (for example, all in millions).

Example: R&D as a percentage of net sales -> "34550 / 416161 * 100".

Limits: at most 500 characters, exponents up to 64, and values up to 1e21 in absolute value.
Division by zero or an expression outside the allow-list returns an error that says what to fix.

## web_search
Search the web for current or external information that is not in Apple's 10-K Filing.

Use it for: current stock prices and market capitalisation, competitor figures (for example Microsoft's latest-quarter gross margin), recent news and events, and anything dated after the Filing's fiscal year. Do not use it for facts the Filing itself reports (Apple's own risk factors, revenue, R&D spend and other fiscal-year figures) - search the Filing for those.

Write a specific query that names the company, the metric and the period, such as "Microsoft gross margin fiscal 2026 Q1". Returns up to 5 results, each with url, title, content (an excerpt) and published_date (null when the source gives none).

Prefer primary sources - investor-relations pages, SEC filings and earnings releases - over aggregators and commentary. When you use a result, always state the period the figure covers (quarter or fiscal year) and cite its URL and published date. If the results do not state the period clearly, say so rather than guessing.

## search_filing
Search Apple's 10-K Filing and return the most relevant Passages, best first. Use it for anything the Filing itself reports: Apple's risk factors, business description, results of operations, and fiscal-year figures such as revenue, gross margin or R&D expense. Do not use it for current market data, competitors or events after the fiscal year - use web_search for those.

Each Passage is a whole unit of the Filing (one subsection, one risk factor, one Note or one table) with passage_id, section_path, page, page_approximate, kind (section, risk_factor, note or table) and content in Markdown. Cite a Passage by its section_path and page.

Scope the search with item whenever you know where the answer lives - it removes off-topic Passages from other parts of the Filing:
- "1A" Risk Factors: principal risks and uncertainties (one Passage per risk factor).
- "7" MD&A: management's explanation of results, gross margin and operating expense drivers, liquidity.
- "8" Financial Statements and Notes: the reported figures (statements of operations, balance sheets, cash flows) and the Notes.
- "1" Business: products, services, segments, competition, employees.
- Also "1B", "1C", "2"-"6", "7A", "9"-"9C", "10"-"16" and "Front Matter".
Pass item as the bare Item number, e.g. "1A" or "8". For a figure, search Item 8 for the statement and Item 7 for the explanation; omit item only when you do not know which Item holds the answer.

Write query in the Filing's own wording ("research and development expense", "gross margin percentage", "principal risks"), not as a question. top_k is 1-10 (default 5); raise it when you need several risk factors or table rows. A search that finds nothing returns an empty passages list with a "No matching passages" message - try other wording or another item rather than guessing. An unknown item is an error that lists the valid items.

## get_passage
Fetch a Passage by cross-reference. Use it when a Passage points elsewhere ("see Note 5", "Part II, Item 7 under the heading ...") or to open an Item or a Note directly.

reference accepts: "Note <n>" (the whole Note), "Item <id>" (the Item's opening Passage), "Part <I-IV>, Item <id>" (the Part must match the Item), or a passage_id from an earlier result, such as "item-8-021". A Note or Item may carry its title as the Filing writes it, e.g. "Note 5 – Property, Plant and Equipment".

Returns passage (passage_id, section_path, page, page_approximate, kind, content) and related: an outline (passage_id, kind, page, title) of the Passages nested under it - a Note's tables and sub-sections, an Item's subsections or risk factors. A Note's text marks each of its tables as "_[Table: <title>]_"; the related entry with that same title is the table, so fetch its passage_id to read the figures. An unknown Note, Item or form is an error that lists what is accepted.
